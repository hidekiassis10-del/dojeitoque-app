Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

This application is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @dojeitoqueejapao. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of any other person.

Data used: the access token and basic profile information (open id, display name) of the owner's own account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit. They are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used solely to upload and publish the owner's videos.

Data sharing: none. No data is sent to third parties other than TikTok itself.

Revocation: the owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app settings, which makes the stored token useless.
