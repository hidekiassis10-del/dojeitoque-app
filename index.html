Do Jeito Que E - Content Publisher

Personal tool that publishes the short Japanese-learning videos of the channel @dojeitoqueejapao to the owner's own TikTok account. It uses TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic) and the Content Posting API (video.upload, video.publish). It only posts content created and approved by the owner, only to the account that authorized it.
